# Kanto Keepsakes App — User Guide (Android & iOS)

Welcome to the Kanto Keepsakes closed beta! This guide covers installing the
app on your phone and everything you can do with it. Kanto Keepsakes is a
peer-to-peer Pokemon TCG trading board: you post what you **have** and what
you **want**, find matching traders, and negotiate the trade yourselves —
the platform never touches your money.

---

## Installing the app

### iPhone / iPad (iOS) — via TestFlight

During the beta the app is distributed through **TestFlight**, Apple's
official beta-testing app.

1. Install **TestFlight** from the App Store (free, made by Apple).
2. Open the **invite link** you received from Kanto Keepsakes (by email or
   the public beta link) on your phone.
3. Tap **Accept**, then **Install** — Kanto Keepsakes appears on your home
   screen like any other app.
4. When a new beta version ships, TestFlight updates it automatically (or
   tap **Update** in TestFlight).

> Requires iOS 15.1 or later. Found a bug? Take a screenshot in the app —
> TestFlight lets you send it straight to the developer with a note.

### Android — via Google Play (internal testing)

1. Open the **opt-in link** you received from Kanto Keepsakes on your phone
   (you must be signed in to the same Google account that was invited).
2. Tap **Accept invitation / Become a tester**.
3. Follow the link to the **Google Play listing** and tap **Install**.
4. Beta updates arrive through Google Play like normal app updates.

> Requires Android 7.0 or later. If the Play listing says "item not found",
> your Google account isn't on the tester list yet — contact
> kantokeepsakes@gmail.com.

### After the public launch

Once the beta ends, both apps will be available by searching **"Kanto
Keepsakes"** directly on the App Store and Google Play — no invite needed.

## Getting started

### Create an account

1. Open the app and tap **Sign up**.
2. Pick a username (3–20 characters — this is your public trader name),
   enter your email, and choose a password (6+ characters).
3. That's it — you land on the Browse tab, logged in.

Forgot your password? Tap **Forgot password?** on the login screen, enter
your email, and follow the reset link it sends you.

### Allow notifications (recommended)

The first time you log in, the app asks for notification permission. Allow
it — you'll get a push notification when someone makes you an offer, counters
yours, messages you, or completes a trade with you. Traders are spread across
timezones; notifications are how deals actually happen. You can fine-tune
which email notifications you get under **Profile → Edit Profile**.

## The four tabs

### 🃏 Browse

The main feed of listings from traders worldwide.

- Toggle between **WTS** (Want to Sell — they have cards, they want cash or
  trades) and **WTB** (Want to Buy — they're hunting for cards).
- **Search** by title, and filter by **country** to find local traders.
- Scroll — more listings load automatically.
- Every card shows the trader's **haves** (with real card images), their
  **wants**, their country flag, and their star rating.
- Tap any listing to open it. Tap **+ Post** in the header to create your
  own (see below).

### 🔄 Matches

The "find trades for me" tab. It compares the cards on **your** active
listings against everyone else's:

- **They have · you want** — that trader is offering a card on your want
  list.
- **They want · you have** — they're hunting a card you're offering.
- **⇄ Two-way match** — both at once; these are your best trade candidates
  and are ranked first.

You need at least one active listing of your own for matches to appear.

### 💬 Inbox

All your conversations. Unread messages show a yellow badge, and each row
shows which listing the chat is about. Chat is **real-time** — messages
appear instantly while you have the conversation open. Scroll up to load
older messages.

### 👤 Profile

Your account hub:

- **My Listings** — every listing you've posted, including sold and expired
  ones. From here you can **Bump** a listing back to the top of Browse (once
  per 24 hours), **Relist** an expired one, **Edit**, or **Delete**.
- **Saved Listings** — your watchlist (see below).
- **Blocked Users** — manage who you've blocked.
- **View Public Profile** — see your profile as other traders see it:
  reputation, trade count, reviews, active listings.
- **Edit Profile** — change your username or bio, and toggle email
  notifications for offers, messages, and trade updates.
- **Log out**, and at the very bottom the **Danger Zone** — permanently
  delete your account (type `DELETE` to confirm).

## Trading, step by step

### 1. Post a listing

Tap **+ Post** in the Browse tab header:

1. **Country** — where you're trading from (state/province optional). Shown
   on your listing so traders know roughly where you are.
2. **Title** — two short lines: what you **[H]ave** and what you **[W]ant**
   (40 characters each). The preview shows exactly how it will look.
3. **Haves** — tap cards in the card picker to attach real card images.
   Browse by era and set, flip between **EN/JP**, search by name (English
   works for Japanese cards too), or check the **Promos** pill. Toggle
   **Cash** if you're also selling.
4. **Wants** — same picker for cards you're hunting, plus preference chips:
   **Cash · Singles · Graded · Sealed**.
5. **Description** — optional, 300 characters, for condition notes and deal
   preferences.

Listings stay active for 30 days (bump or relist to keep them alive).

### 2. Make and negotiate offers

On someone else's listing:

- **Make Offer** (on WTS) / **Offer to Sell** (on WTB) — write what you're
  proposing.
- The owner can **Accept**, **Decline**, or **Counter** your offer — and you
  can counter back. The thread alternates turns until someone accepts.
- **Message Seller/Buyer** — jump straight into chat to hash out details:
  condition photos, payment method, shipping, meetup.
- **Comments** — every listing has a public comment thread. Ask questions or
  vouch/warn publicly; the listing owner can't delete other people's
  comments.

### 3. Complete the trade and rate each other

Once an offer is **accepted**, a trade status card appears on the listing
for both parties:

1. After you've exchanged cards/payment for real, both of you tap
   **Complete Trade**. (Something went wrong? Use **Dispute** instead and
   the moderation team reviews it.)
2. When both sides confirm, the listing is marked **Sold** and the rating
   step unlocks.
3. Rate each other **1–5 stars** with an optional comment. Ratings are
   double-blind: yours stays hidden until the other trader rates you (or 14
   days pass), so ratings stay honest.

Completed trades and ratings build your **reputation** — the number every
trader checks before dealing with you.

### 4. Stay safe

- Check the other trader's **profile** first: trade count, rating, reviews,
  account age.
- Ask for **timestamped photos** of the real card; verify slab cert numbers
  with the grader.
- Keep negotiation **in the chat** so there's a record.
- Use **tracked shipping**; meet in public places for local trades.
- Full guide: [kantokeepsakes.com/safe-trading](https://kantokeepsakes.com/safe-trading)

## Other features

| Feature | How |
|---|---|
| **Save a listing** | Tap ☆ **Save** on any listing detail; find it later under Profile → Saved Listings |
| **View a trader's profile** | Tap their name anywhere (listing, comment, chat) |
| **Report** a listing or user | **Report** button on the listing detail — pick a reason, moderation reviews every report |
| **Block** a user | **Block** on their profile or a listing detail; they can no longer message you or offer on your listings. Unblock under Profile → Blocked Users |
| **Bump** a listing | Profile → My Listings → Bump (every 24h) — back to the top of Browse |
| **Delete your account** | Profile → Danger Zone → type `DELETE`. Permanent — see [kantokeepsakes.com/delete-account](https://kantokeepsakes.com/delete-account) |

## FAQ

**Is there any payment inside the app?**
No. Kanto Keepsakes never handles money. If cash is part of a deal, you and
the other trader agree on the method in chat. There are no fees, no ads, and
no gambling of any kind.

**Why do card images look official?**
They are — cards are picked from real TCG databases (English and Japanese
sets, promos, graded slabs), so listings are unambiguous about exactly which
card is being traded.

**The website asks for a password?**
During the closed beta the website is staging-gated. The app is the primary
way to use Kanto Keepsakes right now; the site opens to everyone at public
launch.

**Something's broken / I have feedback.**
iOS: send it through TestFlight (screenshot → share as beta feedback).
Either platform: email **kantokeepsakes@gmail.com**. Beta feedback directly
shapes what gets fixed before launch.
